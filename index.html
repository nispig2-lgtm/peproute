The model

Two routes for two buyer realities.

PepRoute runs Stock Route tiers for 1, 10, and 50 kit orders, plus a Custom Batch route for 100+ kit buyers or Group Buy Pools.

"The goal is not to publish supplier cost. The goal is to give buyers a simpler all-in quote while matching the order to the right route: fast stock access or controlled custom production."

One all-in route quote.

The buyer sees one delivered quote before paying. Sourcing, communication, coordination, handling, and eligible delivery support are included in the quoted price rather than billed as a second invoice.

No open costTwo route choicesAll-in quote

Stock Route for under 100 kits.

Small buyers can use existing supplier channels at 1 kit, 10 kits, or 50 kits, with quality support based on route terms and evidence.

Custom Batch starts at 100+ kits.

Large orders and group buys can move through made-to-order production from raw material to finished vials, giving stronger batch consistency and clearer aftersale terms.

Testing stays optional.

Factory COA may not be available by default. Third-party testing can be arranged as buyer-funded or shared-batch testing when the order size supports it.

Group Buy Pool.

Buyers who cannot reach 100+ kits alone can register demand into a pool. Once the pool is close to MOQ, the batch terms, testing option, and timeline are confirmed before payment.
